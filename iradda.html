The origin

Born from a simple observation.

The most meaningful gender-equality research often stays within policy circles — never reaching the women who need it most. There's a gap between what is known and what actually changes.

Iradda was founded by an engineer and a policy researcher with direct ties to India's Prime Minister's Office. We translate institutional knowledge into practical tools that help women move forward — with clarity, confidence and community.

The word Iradda draws from the Sanskrit Sankalpa — the sacred vow to act with purpose. We believe intention, when matched with the right support, becomes unstoppable action.

We're not just another NGO. We're grounded in evidence — backed by research from NITI Aayog — and built by people who understand both the policy architecture and the engineering of systems that scale. We exist in the gap between the two.
