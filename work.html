Biomedical / FEA
2026

StentForge — coronary stent digital twin

OENG1168 Engineering Capstone · Lead author: Vishesh Kumar

A Python pipeline in PyAnsys that designs coronary stents, takes each one through crimp, expansion and release inside a narrowed artery, then studies the blood flow with CFD and ranks the designs on eight objectives. RMIT capstone with LEAP Australia and Ansys.

Our best design reopened the 48 % narrowing to 8 % residual stenosis after release. 38 of the 101 designs reached full expansion; optiSLang predicted the recoil of 26 unseen designs with R² 0.99, and SimAI reproduced the stent's full deformation field with R² 0.985. Ansys Mechanical APDL · Fluent · optiSLang · SimAI · PyMAPDL.

CFD / Turbulence
Oct 2026

Turbulent flow over a backward-facing step

MIET2586 Computational Fluid Dynamics · Individual

The backward-facing step is a well established test case in computational fluid dynamics (CFD). A two-dimensional backward-facing step in a channel was modeled at a Reynolds number based on the step height of Re = 5100 using three different meshing schemes and a three-level refinement strategy. Four turbulence treatments and two fluids were used, and the results were compared with DNS (reattachment length 6.28h) and experimental data (6.0h).

Both k-ε models for the 2D BFS were found to reattach too early, the standard model by 33.5% and the realizable model by 23.0%. ANSYS Fluent · mesh refinement study · k-ε, k-ω SST · validation against DNS and experiment.

Heat transfer / CFD
May 2026

Degrees of Danger: infants left in hot cars

Heat transfer coursework · Group of 5 · My part: the windows-closed CFD simulation

This study models the transient thermal response of a typical car cabin (G-wagon: internal volume ~8 m³) under Melbourne summer conditions (35°C ambient, 15 January) to quantify the time to life-threatening infant core temperatures (identified as 40-42°C). A three-dimensional transient CFD simulation was used to record and compare cabin air temperature over time.

Cabin air temperature reaches approximately 50°C within 15 minutes and approaches a steady-state of ~54°C at 60 minutes. The 40°C heat stroke threshold is projected to be reached within approximately 75 minutes, and a 200 mm window opening provided no meaningful thermal protection. ANSYS Fluent · DO radiation with solar loading · transient.

FEA / Structural
Oct 2025

Suspension shaft rocker and lower wishbone

MIET1084 Finite Element Analysis · Pair project

Finite element analysis of a shaft rocker and lower suspension wishbone has been conducted using ANSYS Workbench for application in a vehicle suspension assembly. Structural analyses were performed on the baseline geometries, followed by iterative geometric refinements to achieve the required safety factors while minimizing overall mass.

Shaft rocker: a minimum fatigue safety factor of 1.74 at a design life of 1000 blocks. Lower wishbone: buckling safety factor of 7.73, maximum deformation of 0.31 mm and minimum yield safety factor of 4.05. Both designs met the 75 g and 300 g mass limits. ANSYS Workbench · SpaceClaim · fatigue · eigenvalue buckling.

UAV / Machine learning
Oct 2026

Hearing propeller damage on a quadrotor

MIET2580 Vehicle Noise and Vibration · Individual

A propeller failure can be dangerous for a quadrotor. Normally, propeller damage is discovered by ground inspection. We used 1956 five-second on-board audio clips from a published data set to classify the number of damaged propellers (0, 1, 2, 3–4), holding out five configurations.

The course-default 1-NN scores 99.2, 98.4 and 84.1%; a pre-registered mel-bank change raises the last to 87.6% (the final model). Detection could trigger inspections; counting is not yet reliable. MATLAB · MFCC features · 1-NN, SVM · flight-grouped validation.

Thermodynamics / Energy
May 2026

Off-grid cogeneration for a wool mill

Energy and exergy analysis · Group of 3 · My part: energy balance and exergy balance

This project evaluated the feasibility of a diesel cogeneration system for supplying both electricity and hot water to a wool mill operating without access to grid electricity or mains gas. The system uses a diesel generator to meet the mill's electrical demand, while recovering waste heat from the engine coolant and exhaust gas to heat process water.

An 18 kW generator was selected for a 17.78 kW peak demand. The exergy analysis showed that the diesel generator had the largest irreversibility in the system; the coolant heat exchanger achieved a maximum exergetic efficiency of 60.3%. Energy balance · exergy analysis · storage sizing.
